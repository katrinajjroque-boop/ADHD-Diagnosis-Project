### Model fit

The original analysis reported likelihood-ratio/deviance p-values above 0.05 for all three full-versus-intercept-only comparisons:

- Logistic: deviance 9.0488, p = 0.24917
- Probit: deviance 8.9946, p = 0.25304
- Complementary log-log: deviance 9.3872, p = 0.22604

### Predictor significance

Anxiety was the only predictor reported as statistically significant at the 0.05 level in the model outputs.

These results should be interpreted in the context of the small sample and the selected variables. They do not establish clinical diagnostic validity.
